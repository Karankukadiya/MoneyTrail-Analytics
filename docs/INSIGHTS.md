# MoneyTrail Analytics — Insights

## 1. Overall Financial Health

- The platform processed **5,000 transactions** totaling **₹349.94M** in
  combined volume, with **92.96% success rate** (4,648 successful,
  352 failed).
- **Total Balance sits at +₹2.93M** — Inflows (₹176.44M) and Outflows
  (₹173.50M) are nearly matched, indicating a platform in a stable,
  mature phase rather than aggressive growth or decline.
- **Average transaction value is ₹75,289**, pulled up by high-ticket
  loan and deposit products (see Product section).
- Transaction volume is **accelerating sharply**: 654 transactions in
  2023 → 1,209 in 2024 → 3,137 in 2025 (a ~4.8x increase from 2023 to
  2025), showing strong platform adoption growth year over year.

---

## 2. Transaction Behavior

**Channel usage** (5,000 total transactions):

| Channel | Volume | Share |
|---|---|---|
| UPI | 2,014 | 40.3% |
| Mobile App | 1,299 | 26.0% |
| Net Banking | 740 | 14.8% |
| ATM | 487 | 9.7% |
| Branch | 236 | 4.7% |
| POS | 224 | 4.5% |

- UPI + Mobile App together account for **66.3%** of all transaction
  volume — confirming a digitally-first user base with minimal reliance
  on physical branches (branch usage is just 4.7%).

**Failure rate by channel:**

| Channel | Failure Rate |
|---|---|
| Net Banking | 7.97% |
| ATM | 7.80% |
| UPI | 7.40% |
| POS | 6.70% |
| Mobile App | 6.16% |
| Branch | 4.66% |

- **Net Banking and ATM have the highest failure rates** (~8%), while
  **Branch transactions are the most reliable** (4.66%) — likely because
  in-person transactions have fewer technical points of failure than
  digital channels.
- Despite UPI's dominant volume, its 7.40% failure rate is close to the
  platform average (7.04%) — not a standout risk on its own, but worth
  monitoring given its scale (a 1-point improvement in UPI's failure
  rate would recover more failed transactions than fixing any other
  channel).

## 3. Customer Insights

- **800 total customers**, segmented as: Retail (562, 70.3%), Premium
  (182, 22.8%), Corporate (56, 7.0%) — a typical retail-heavy fintech
  customer base.
- **Regional distribution:** South (185) and West (168) hold the most
  customers; Central (128) has the fewest — a ~31% gap between the
  largest and smallest regional customer base.
- **Top 10 customers** each generated 26-32 transactions — well above
  the platform average of 6.25 transactions/customer (5,000 ÷ 800),
  indicating a small but highly active power-user segment.
- **Customer transaction activity has grown steadily**, from ~40
  transactions/month in early 2023 to 180+/month by 2025 — consistent
  with the overall volume growth trend.

## 4. Product Performance

**By category (Net = Inflows − Outflows, Success-only transactions):**

| Category | Inflows | Outflows | Net |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deposits | ₹42.34M | ₹37.50M | **+₹4.84M** |
| Credit | ₹115.28M | ₹115.31M | -₹0.03M |
| Investments | ₹10.75M | ₹11.13M | -₹0.38M |
| Insurance | ₹3.65M | ₹3.68M | -₹0.03M |
| Payments | ₹4.42M | ₹5.88M | -₹1.46M |

- **Deposits is the only category with a meaningfully positive net
  balance** (+₹4.84M) — this single category is effectively carrying
  the platform's entire positive Total Balance.
- **Credit is nearly perfectly balanced** (-₹0.03M net) despite being by
  far the largest category by volume (₹230M+ combined) — loans and
  credit cards bring in almost as much in repayments/related credit
  activity as they pay out.
- **Payments is the most outflow-heavy category** (-₹1.46M net) — makes
  sense, since UPI Wallet, Prepaid Card, and Debit Card transactions are
  primarily spending activity.

**Top individual products:**
- **Personal Loan** is the top inflow-generating product (₹38.40M)
- **Recurring Deposit** has the lowest outflow (₹1.57M) — the platform's
  most cash-retentive product
- Loan products (Home/Auto/Personal) and Fixed Deposit each average
  ₹250K+ per transaction, roughly **8x higher** than everyday products
  like UPI Wallet or cards (~₹30K average)

## 5. Account Health

- **1,000 total accounts**: 849 open (84.9%), 151 closed (15.1%).
- **Account type is evenly split** across Savings (238), Current (254),
  Salary (252), and NRI (256) — no single account type dominates.

**Account lifecycle by year:**

| Year | Opened | Closed | Net Growth | Closure Rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 177 | 15 | +162 | 8.5% |
| 2024 | 278 | 38 | +240 | 13.7% |
| 2025 | 308 | 93 | +215 | 30.2% |

- **Openings are still growing** (177 → 278 → 308) but **growth is
  decelerating** (+57% from 2023→2024, only +11% from 2024→2025).
- **Closures are accelerating much faster than openings**: the closure
  rate (closed ÷ opened in the same year) jumped from 8.5% in 2023 to
  30.2% in 2025 — a **3.5x increase**. This is the single most important
  trend in the account data: net account growth is still positive, but
  the *retention* side of the business is weakening while acquisition
  growth is flattening.

**Regional balance breakdown:**

| Region | Net Balance |
|---|---|
| East | +₹5.91M |
| North | +₹3.25M |
| South | -₹0.52M |
| Central | -₹1.36M |
| West | -₹4.35M |

- East and North are the only regions carrying a positive net balance;
  West is the most negative (-₹4.35M), despite having the second-highest
  customer count (168) — suggesting West-region customers skew toward
  higher borrowing/spending relative to deposits compared to other
  regions.
